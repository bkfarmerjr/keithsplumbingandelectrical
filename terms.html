SMS Notification Program — Keith's Plumbing & Electrical

Effective Date: July 1, 2025  |  Last Updated: July 1, 2025

1. Program Description

Program Name: Keith's Plumbing & Electrical SMS Notification Program

Keith's Plumbing & Electrical LLC operates a transactional SMS notification program to keep customers informed about their service appointments and job status. This program is strictly for customer-care and service-related communications. No marketing or promotional messages are sent through this program.

2. Message Types

By opting in, you may receive the following types of SMS messages:

  • Service appointment scheduling confirmations
  • Technician dispatch / "on the way" notifications
  • Job completion notices
  • Invoice sent notifications
  • Estimate follow-up messages
  • Appointment reminders

3. Message Frequency

Message frequency varies and depends on the number of active service jobs associated with your account. You may receive up to 4–6 messages per service job. We will not send unsolicited or bulk messages.

4. Message & Data Rates

Msg & data rates may apply. Standard carrier message and data rates from your mobile provider may apply to messages sent and received as part of this program. Contact your wireless carrier for details about your plan.

5. How to Opt In

You may opt in to this SMS program by:

  • Completing the written opt-in form at: keithsplumbingandelectrical — SMS Opt-In
  • Providing verbal consent when contacting us by phone to schedule service, at which point you will be informed of the program and your right to opt out

6. How to Opt Out — STOP

You may cancel your SMS subscription at any time. Reply STOP to any message you receive from us. After texting STOP, you will receive one final confirmation message and no further messages will be sent. To re-enroll, complete the opt-in form or contact us directly.
