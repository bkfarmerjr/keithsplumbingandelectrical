Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information

Effective Date: July 1, 2025  |  Last Updated: July 1, 2025

1. Who We Are

Keith's Plumbing & Electrical LLC ("we," "us," or "our") is a licensed plumbing and electrical service company located at 245 Ashby Rd, Camden, TN 38320. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect and use information when you contact us for service, visit our website, or opt in to our SMS notification program.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you request service or opt in to SMS communications:

  • Full name
  • Mobile phone number
  • Email address
  • Service address
  • Date and time of opt-in consent
  • Nature of the service requested

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to provide and communicate about the services you have requested. Specifically, we use your mobile phone number only to send the following transactional SMS messages:

  • Service appointment confirmation and scheduling updates
  • Technician dispatch notifications ("on the way" alerts)
  • Job completion notices
  • Invoice delivery notifications
  • Estimate follow-up messages

We do not use your information for bulk marketing, promotional campaigns, or any purpose unrelated to your service request.

4. SMS Messaging Program

By opting in to our SMS program, you consent to receive transactional text messages from Keith's Plumbing & Electrical. Message frequency varies based on your active service jobs. Msg & data rates may apply. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. For help, reply HELP or contact us directly.

5. We Do Not Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information — including your phone number — with any third parties for marketing or any other commercial purpose. Your information is used only for the direct delivery of services you have requested from us.

We may share information only when required by law or to comply with a valid legal process.

6. Data Retention

We retain opt-in records (name, phone number, consent timestamp) as required to demonstrate compliance with carrier and regulatory requirements. You may request deletion of your information at any time by contacting us.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP
  • Request that we delete your personal information
  • Ask what information we hold about you

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the information below.

8. Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect your information from unauthorized access or disclosure. We do not store financial information such as credit card numbers.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
